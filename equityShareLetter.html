5 Equity Vesting and Contribution Terms

To align long-term incentives and safeguard enterprise stability, the 15% equity participation shall follow an agreed vesting and performance framework. The definitive documentation shall formally establish:

  • Whether the 15% interest is allotted upfront subject to clawback, or earned progressively over an agreed timeline.
  • Objective milestone thresholds tied to business expansion, financial milestones, or operational deliverables.
  • Vesting commencement date, cliff periods, and quarterly/annual vesting increments.
  • Clear legal treatment of unvested equity in the event of voluntary departure, termination for cause, or retirement.
Proposed Framework: Four-year vesting schedule with a one-year cliff (25% vesting after 12 months, followed by equal quarterly vesting over 36 months), or alternative milestone structure as mutually agreed in writing.

6 Rights and Benefits of the Equity Partner

Upon valid allotment and statutory registration, the partner shall enjoy customary shareholder rights attached to their equity class:

A. Ownership Rights

Unencumbered legal ownership of the allotted 15% equity interest, subject to constitutional documents.

B. Pro-Rata Dividend Rights

Entitlement to receive dividends proportionate to a 15% shareholding whenever lawful dividends are declared and distributed by the Board.

C. Voting & Governance Rights

Statutory voting rights in general meetings proportionate to shareholding, as governed by the Articles of Association.

D. Strategic Business Participation

Direct participation in senior leadership deliberations, quarterly business appraisals, and strategic decision forums.

E. Financial Transparency

Periodic access to unaudited quarterly financial summaries, annual audited statements, and major corporate development updates.

7 Future Funding and Dilution Principles

As Pixelix scales, capital expansion, strategic venture funding, debt syndication, or issuance of ESOP pools may be warranted. Under such circumstances:

  • The partner's 15% holding shall be subject to proportionate, equitable dilution alongside all existing promoters and shareholders.
  • The partner shall be granted customary pre-emptive rights to participate in future rounds on equal footing (pari passu) to preserve percentage holding.
  • The 15% offer does not constitute a permanent, non-dilutable stake in the event of third-party external investment.

8 Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

The equity partner shall hold in strict fiduciary trust all proprietary algorithms, client rosters, financial models, custom source code, brand assets, and trade secrets belonging to Pixelix.

All works, inventions, intellectual properties, and designs generated in connection with Pixelix services shall vest solely and perpetually with Pixelix under a standard IP Assignment Agreement.

9 Exit, Transfer, ROFR & Withdrawal Governance

To preserve company cohesion, equity shares cannot be pledged, mortgaged, sold, or transferred to third parties without triggering the statutory Right of First Refusal (ROFR) in favor of existing promoters.

The definitive SHA shall clearly codify buyback provisions, tag-along/drag-along rights, valuation formulas certified by registered valuers, and clear mechanisms governing retirement, disability, or breach of obligations.

10 Remuneration & Independent Compensation

The proposed 15% equity participation represents an ownership stake in the enterprise and is independent of any salary, consulting stipend, performance commission, or out-of-pocket reimbursements. Operating compensation shall be agreed upon and documented via a separate employment or consultancy contract.

11 Corporate Governance & Reserved Matters

Major corporate transactions—including alterations to share capital, onboarding outside venture investors, mergers, or material asset liquidations—shall be subject to affirmative voting covenants defined in the Shareholders' Agreement. A 15% equity stake does not automatically confer an independent veto over routine day-to-day operations.
